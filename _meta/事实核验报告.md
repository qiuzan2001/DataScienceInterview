## 3. 已核验：库内表述**不精确或错误**（必须改）

| # | 库内表述 | 问题 | 权威原文 / 正确写法 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **E1** ⛔ | `1.6 Estimation Issues & Separation.md`：分离问题的解决方案列 **Penalized Regression (Ridge/Lasso)** 作为对策 | **误导**。PSL 明确指出惩罚**不能解决**完全分离 | S1a §10.3.1 原文：<br>"It's important to clarify that using regularization techniques such as Ridge or Lasso **won't resolve the convergence issue** encountered in this specific scenario… **cross-validation will reveal that the optimal choice for the regularization parameter (lambda) is zero**. This underscores that the primary problem isn't related to overfitting…"<br>根因："when the true classification rule is flawless… the model parameters become undefined, tending toward ±∞"<br>✅ 正确写法：Ridge/Lasso 只能**缓解**（CV 可能选出 λ=0 而失效）；真正给出**有限估计**的是 **Firth 惩罚**（`ℓ(β)+½log det I(β)`，即 Jeffreys prior，R 包 `logistf`）或**贝叶斯先验**。<br>**另有一处库内**也值得注意：PSL 同时强调"this doesn't necessarily invalidate the model's usefulness for making predictions, as **the decision boundary remains constant**"——即分离主要破坏**参数推断**，对**预测**影响有限。这一 nuance 库内缺失。 |
| **E2** ⛔ | `1.4 Model Fitting.md`：断言把 LSE 用于 logistic 会 "inefficient and biased" | **理由不准确** | S1a §10.1 给出的权威理由是**损失尺度/梯度**问题，不是「有偏」：<br>"While one might consider using the L2 loss `(y−η(x))²`, it has some limitations. Specifically, it **tends to be too flat for training** because the quantity `(y−η(x))` is typically less than 1. Squaring it further reduces the scale of the loss, **resulting in small gradients, making training difficult** and potentially leading to getting stuck at certain points. Instead, we opt for the log-likelihood as our loss function."<br>✅ 正确写法：① L2 在 0/1 目标上尺度太小→梯度过平、优化困难；② LSE 对二值因变量不是良定义的估计目标（概率可能越界）；③ 用对数似然（MLE）当损失，统计性质更优（一致性、有效性、渐近正态）。 |
| **E3** ⚠️ | WOE 符号约定**三处互相冲突**：`2. Transformations.md`（写成 `ln(P(X\|Y=1)/P(X\|Y=0))` 却标 Goods/Bads，算例实算 `%Events/%Non-Events`）、`2.3.1 WOE & IV.md`（用 `%non-events/%events`）、`5.2 Univariate Selection.md`（用 `Goods/Bads`） | **同一指标在库内有三种方向**，会导致「正 WOE = 高风险还是低风险」结论相反 | ✅ 统一为学分卡主流约定：<br>`WOE_k = ln( %Goods / %Bads ) = ln( P(X=k\|Y=Good) / P(X=k\|Y=Bad) )`<br>其中 Good = 非事件（Y=0），Bad = 事件（Y=1）。<br>⇒ **WOE 为正 = 好客户占比更高 = 低风险**；`IV = Σ(%Goods − %Bads)·WOE`。<br>说明：不同教材把 Good/Bad 与事件/非事件的定义互换，**方向取决于约定**，关键是**全库一致**并在每处显式声明。 |
| **E4** ⚠️ | `8. Ensemble Learning.md` 内部冲突：正文（约 L107）"Reduces both bias and variance"，同文对比表（约 L158）Boosting 的 Variance 列填 `No`；`8.6` 又说多轮后方差上升 | 同一问题三个口径 | 权威表述（S1b §12.4 末段 + S1c §4.3）：<br>"The final classifier obtained from AdaBoost may not perform well on test data… **potentially leading to overfitting. Proper regularization or early stopping is often necessary**."<br>✅ 统一写法：**Boosting 主要降低偏差**；对方差的影响**取决于正则化与早停**——学习率小 + 树浅 + 早停时方差可控甚至下降；**轮数过多且无正则时方差会上升**。 |
| **E5** ⚠️ | AdaBoost 表述若写成「训练误差随迭代**单调下降**」 | **不精确** | S1b §12.4 原文明确：<br>"It's important to note that the **training error of the combined classifier G_T generated by AdaBoost is not guaranteed to be monotonically decreasing** with respect to the number of iterations. Instead, after each iteration, AdaBoost decreases a specific **upper bound** on the 0/1 training error."<br>✅ 正确写法：每轮下降的是**上界** `∏2√(ε_t(1−ε_t))`，实际 0/1 训练误差不保证单调。 |
| **E6** ⚠️ | `7.2. Building the Forest.md` 复杂度自相矛盾：L145 `O(B·m·n·(log n)²)` vs L201 `O(B×n×m×log n)` | 相差一个 log n 因子 | PSL 未给统一复杂度结论（其 §4.x 未列该表）。✅ 处理建议：**改为不写单一复杂度公式**，只说明「训练成本 ≈ 树数 B × 每棵树成本（与 n log n 级的排序/扫描、候选特征数 m 相关）」，避免给出无出处的精确阶。 |

## 7. 结论摘要

- **库内技术内容整体质量高**：AdaBoost 上界、CCP + 1-SE、RF 的 m 值、bootstrap 63.2%、前向分步等**核心公式都与 PSL 一致**。
